Berka Text

A prose-first Berka cut with quasi-proportional spacing, taller reading rhythm, true italic emphasis, and a quietly rectangular texture.

Paragraph Texture

The blog page is a quiet machine for thought. It should hold long sentences without turning them into code, let emphasis feel natural, and keep numbers like 2026, initials like API, and marks like ?! clear at reading speed.

Essay Body

Berka Text keeps the rectangular Berka voice, but the rhythm loosens. Narrow letters get their own space, wide letters stop forcing the whole line into a grid, and paragraphs feel written instead of assembled.

Italic Voice

The italic is meant for emphasis inside an article: present enough to be noticed, calm enough to survive repeated use in a long review, note, or technical essay.
