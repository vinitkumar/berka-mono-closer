Field Note

A quieter page for longer thoughts

June 24, 2026 · 5 minute read

The blog page is a quiet machine for thought. It should hold long sentences without turning them into code, let emphasis feel natural, and keep numbers, initials, and punctuation clear at reading speed.

Berka Text keeps the rectangular Berka voice, but the rhythm loosens: narrow letters get their own space, wide letters stop forcing every line into the same grid, and paragraphs feel written instead of assembled.

Good prose type does not disappear by becoming bland. It disappears by making the sentence easier to trust.

The result should still feel related to Berka Mono Focus in code blocks, but the body copy now has its own texture, pace, and editorial voice.
